UI/UX Case Study Product Concept

Nanora

Trusted care. Meaningful learning.

A two-sided childcare platform connecting families with vetted nanny-tutors and specialized caregivers. Grounded in UNICEF Early Childhood Development frameworks to make childcare search safe, transparent, and manageable.

Project Overview

Nanora is an end-to-end UX/UI concept spanning both responsive web and mobile application touchpoints. The platform bridges the gap between working parents seeking reliable, vetted childcare and qualified educators looking for fair, dignified caregiver opportunities.

Role

Lead UX/UI Designer

Deliverables

User research, sitemap, 5-step journey, visual identity

Tools

Figma, Miro, Adobe Illustrator

Problem Statement

Childcare is not an ordinary consumer purchase—it is an emotionally vulnerable decision. Parents struggle with opaque vetting procedures, lack of trust in online listings, and the difficulty of finding certified caregivers for specialized needs (developmental delays, respite care).

“Trust first, clarity second, convenience third.” — Core Product Principle

Core Offerings

To eliminate ambiguity, Nanora simplifies care into three clear service categories:

  • Nanny-Tutors: Childcare seamlessly paired with age-appropriate early learning (cognitive activities, reading, emotional development).
  • Specialized Care: Certified support and respite care for children with disabilities, neurodivergence, or specific healthcare needs.
  • Respite Care: Flexible, dependable temporary support giving families essential relief during demanding schedules.

5-Step User Journey

1

Need Intake

Child age, care type, location, schedule, and specialized requirements

2

Discovery

Granular filtering by vetted credentials, background checks, and availability

3

Compare

Detailed profiles with parent reviews, verified badges, and care philosophy

4

Book

Real-time booking requests and secure communication

5

Manage

Shared calendar, care notes, daily check-ins, and support
